Services

How can we help?

Immediate IRS Collection Enforcement

When tax liabilities go unaddressed, the IRS escalates from sending warning letters to active enforcement.

Form 941 Payroll Tax Defaults & Trust Fund Penalties

For small-to-mid-sized business owners, unremitted payroll taxes represent a severe corporate and personal crisis.

Accumulated Back Taxes and Missing Returns

Many taxpayers seek resolution help after years of "tax paralysis," where a single unfiled return or minor debt snowballs out of control due to compounding interest and penalties.

Offers in Compromise (OIC)

Structuring, calculating, and submitting settlements to resolve tax debts for a fraction of what is owed based on your Reasonable Collection Potential (RCP).

Installment Agreements (IA)

Negotiating complex collection payment plans, including Partial Payment Installment Agreements (PPIAs) and Streamlined Agreements, ensuring monthly payments are financially sustainable for you.

Penalty Abatements

Draft petitions for the removal of failure-to-file and failure-to-pay penalties based on First-Time Abatement (FTA) criteria or Reasonable Cause arguments.

Investigation & Compliance Baseline

The Blueprint
Financial Strategy & Case Architecture

We perform deep-dive financial asset profiling and complete back-tax filing requirements.


Deliverables: Formulating your definitive defense portfolio and choosing your optimal path out of debt (OIC vs. Installment Agreement).

The Resolution
Formal IRS Submission & Negotiation

We formally compile, structure, and submit your resolution packet directly to IRS and State authorities.


Deliverables: Aggressive, expert-led negotiation through appeals to secure your permanent settlement or sustainable installment plan.
